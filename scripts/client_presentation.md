# Executive Summary

### Autonomous Voice & CRM Platform

* **Deployment Target:** Installation 1 (100% On-Premise Infrastructure).
* **Live Inbound Telephony:** Active and answering customer calls on **Extension 8000**.
* **Zero Cloud Dependency:** Complete data sovereignty. All voice processing, intelligence, and databases operate strictly within the client's internal network.
* **Core Functionality:** Handles customer inquiries, conducts multi-turn complaint registration, generates official tracking tickets, and logs customer satisfaction ratings.

---

# What the Platform Delivers Today

### Live Capabilities on Extension 8000

* **Instant Call Answering:** 24/7 immediate call pickup with zero waiting or queue delays.
* **Natural Voice Communication:** Speaks naturally in **English**, **Hindi**, and regional **Odia**.
* **Instant Policy & Banking Resolution:** Answers routine customer queries (ATM limits, card blocking, fee schedules) in under 1 second.
* **Structured Complaint Intake:** Automatically guides callers to capture **State, City, Address, and Complaint Details**.
* **Real-Time Ticket Issuance:** Generates official tracking IDs (`CMP-XXXXXX`) and writes directly to the enterprise database.
* **Complaint Status Tracking:** Callers can quote their ticket ID to receive instant real-time progress updates.
* **Customer Feedback:** Automatically captures 1–5 star post-call satisfaction ratings.

# Enterprise Security & Data Privacy

### 100% On-Premise Guarantee

* **Zero Data Leaves Premises:** All customer speech, transcripts, and records remain strictly on-site.
* **No Third-Party AI APIs:** Operates independently without relying on external cloud providers.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Adheres to strict banking, financial, and government data privacy standards.
* **Offline Protection:** Built-in resilience ensures zero lost tickets even during momentary internal database drops.
* **24/7 Availability:** Continuous background monitoring ensures speech and reasoning engines remain active with zero warm-up delay.
