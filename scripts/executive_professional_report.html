1. Executive Summary

This report provides the formal operational status for Installation 1 of the Com-One Autonomous Voice and CRM Platform. Inbound telephony integration is fully deployed and verified on Extension 8000 via Asterisk PBX. The platform independently ingests customer calls, provides sub-second policy clarification via semantic retrieval, conducts multi-turn structured complaint registration with comprehensive Indian geographic normalization, and synchronizes real-time incident records directly with the enterprise database.

A background health and warmup daemon operates continuously on a 120-second heartbeat cycle, ensuring memory persistence of all speech models and database connection pools to eliminate cold-start latency.

3. Core Operational Capabilities
Key Operational Highlights:
  • Multilingual Natural Synthesis: Pre-rendered and dynamic neural voice delivery supporting English, Hindi, and native Odia.

  • Fault-Tolerant Persistence: Local resilience buffering guarantees zero lost customer complaints even during momentary database disconnection.

5. Phase 2 Milestone Roadmap

With Phase 1 core telephony, cognitive NLU, neural synthesis, and CRM persistence fully operational, Phase 2 engineering is structured around human-in-the-loop escalation and supervisory management:

  • SIP Human Queue Transfer: Seamless bridging from the autonomous AI agent to human agent extensions upon explicit request or negative sentiment escalation.
  • Supervisor Real-Time Monitoring: Deployment of a secure, internal web console providing live call queue metrics, sentiment analysis, and verbatim transcripts.
  • Caller Recognition & Session Recall: Automated cross-session profile retrieval linking incoming caller numbers to existing CRM complaint history.
  • Automated PII Redaction: In-line masking filters for national identification and payment credentials prior to database archival.
