# The Big Picture

### What Is This Platform?

* An **Autonomous AI Phone Agent** that answers incoming customer calls like a trained human receptionist.
* **Solves routine questions on the spot** (ATM limits, blocking cards, bank charges).
* **Takes down customer complaints step-by-step** and saves them directly into the company database.
* **Gives real tracking ticket numbers** (`CMP-XXXXXX`) so customers can check their status anytime.
* **100% Private:** Operates entirely inside the office network. Zero customer audio or records ever leave the building.

# 100% In-House & Private (On-Premise)

### Why This Matters for Security & Banking

* **Zero Cloud Dependency:** The AI does not use external cloud services (no OpenAI, no Google Cloud, no third-party APIs).

# What Happens When a Customer Calls

### The Exact Call Experience on Extension 8000

1. **Instant Pickup:** The AI answers immediately with zero hold time.
2. **Friendly Human Voice:** Speaks clearly in English, Hindi, or native regional Odia.
3. **Answers Questions Fast:** Explains banking policies and rules in under 1 second.
4. **Step-by-Step Complaint Intake:**
   * *"Which State are you calling from?"*
   * *"Which City?"* (Recognizes Mumbai, Delhi, Bhubaneswar, etc.)
   * *"What is your Address?"*
   * *"Please describe the problem you are facing."*
5. **Issues Tracking Ticket:** Hands the customer their tracking ID (e.g. `CMP-729415`).
6. **Checks Complaint Status:** Callers can quote their ticket ID to hear real-time updates.
7. **Star Rating Survey:** Asks for a 1 to 5 star rating before saying goodbye.

# How the AI Works (In Simple Terms)

### 4 Cognitive Steps Behind Every Call

* **1. The Ears (Speech Understanding):**
  Listens to the caller, filters out background noise, and understands different Indian accents.
* **2. The Brain (Instant Knowledge):**
  Knows all banking rules, ATM limits, and all 28 Indian States & major cities.
* **3. The Voice (Natural Talking):**
  Speaks back in a natural human voice in under 0.1 seconds using pre-rendered audio.
* **4. The Hands (Filing Complaints):**
  Directly opens the database and types in the customer's complaint without human help.

# Recent Fixes & Improvements

### What We Solved in the Latest Update

* **Fixed the "Mumbai Loop":** Previously, saying a city name like *"Mumbai"* caused the AI to mistakenly say *"Have a wonderful day"*. This is completely fixed.
* **Fixed Speech Glitches:** Eliminated phantom *"Thank you"* loops on short words.
* **Learned the Indian Map:** Added all 28 States and 50+ major cities so the AI never gets confused by locations.
* **Consistent Voice Persona:** Fixed the voice so it sounds like the exact same person throughout the entire phone call.
* **Offline Protection (Zero Data Loss):** If the database connection ever blinks during a call, complaints are saved locally and synced automatically once reconnected.
