4. What Was Fixed & Improved Recently

  • Fixed the "Mumbai Loop": Previously, telling the AI a city name like *"Mumbai"* caused it to mistakenly say *"Have a wonderful day"*. This has been completely solved; the AI now smoothly proceeds to ask for the address and problem.
  • Fixed Speech Stutter: Eliminated false *"Thank you"* loops on short words.
  • Learned the Map of India: Taught the AI all 28 States, 8 Union Territories, and 50+ major cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar, etc.).
  • Unified Voice Persona: The AI now sounds like the exact same person throughout the entire phone call.
  • Offline Safety Guarantee: If the database connection ever blinks during a call, complaints are saved locally and automatically synced once the connection is restored (zero lost complaints).
