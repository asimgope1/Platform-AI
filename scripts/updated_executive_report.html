A 120-second background heartbeat daemon ensures zero cold-start delay across speech, reasoning, and database components.

2. Support & Maintenance Methodology (Installation 1)

C. Sentiment & Frustration Scoring

Multi-turn acoustic and lexical sentiment tracking. Detects customer agitation early to trigger escalation protocols before frustration peaks.

3. Operational Challenges & Strategic Mitigations
Challenge Domain Operational Risk / Real-World Friction Implemented Mitigation Strategy
On-Premise Compute Local hardware capacity constraints can increase latency under high call concurrency. Dual-Brain Architecture: Fast-path mini-model handles 80% of calls in <100ms; heavy model quantized for sub-second execution.
8kHz Telephony Audio Bandwidth limits and background noise degrade speech accuracy on Indian names/cities. Geographic Normalization & VAD: Pre-loaded dictionary covering 28 States & 50+ Cities; noise-conditioned audio thresholding.
Sentiment False-Positives Culturally expressive or loud speech can be misclassified as angry, causing premature transfer. Multi-Turn Moving Average: Requires consecutive negative sentiment turns and semantic confirmation before escalating.
Policy & Knowledge Drift Banking policies and fee structures change frequently, risking outdated AI responses. Versioned Single-Source RAG: Structured knowledge tagging with strict hierarchical precedence and effective date stamping.
Zero-Downtime Patching Applying model or code patches on active PBX servers risks dropping in-flight calls. Graceful Worker Reloads: Blue/green rolling process reloads ensure active calls on Extension 8000 complete uninterrupted.
